# 2 · Persona & point of view

*Working persona – a hypothesis to validate, not a real person.*

## Lena, 38 – Recruiting Operations Lead, SAP HR

> "I built agents for everything. Nobody tells me whether they still need to be agents."

- Runs high-volume recruiting admin: application status updates, document checks, interview scheduling, offer letters
- **Builds her own automations** with the tools she already has – Microsoft Copilot Studio, Power Automate, n8n – agents included
- Was trained on the tool, never on when to use which step
- **Owns her automations end to end** – nobody else reviews them

| | |
|---|---|
| **Goals** | Less manual admin for her team, faster hiring, show that recruiting innovates |
| **Pains** | No guidance on which step fits; no time to rebuild what already runs |
| **Behaviour** | Reaches for an agent by default – "everyone is building agents" |
| **Blind spot** | Never sees what her agents consume, and has no reason to choose lean |

## Customer vs user

- **Customer:** the SAP **CIO office** – owns AI tool licences, IT spend and AI governance; commissioned the taskforce that built rAIght
- **User:** **process owners** like Lena – their behaviour rAIght has to change
- **Stakeholder:** the sustainability office (needs the CO₂ figures for reporting)

## Point of view

> **Lena**, a process owner who builds her own automations, **needs** a simple way to choose and keep the leanest automation that does the job, **because** no one else checks it – and without guidance, visibility and a reason, she defaults to agents.

## How might we …

| Question | Becomes |
|---|---|
| … help Lena pick the right step herself? | Training + triage |
| … show her what her agents really use? | Dashboard |
| … make switching to a workflow effortless? | Guided conversion |
| … give her a reason to choose lean? | Carbon budget per unit, recognition |

## Out of scope

Choosing candidates is a **human decision** and deliberately out of scope (high-risk under the EU AI Act). rAIght only covers recruiting **admin** processes.
